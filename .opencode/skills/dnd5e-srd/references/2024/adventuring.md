# Exploration and Travel (SRD 5.2 / 2024 rules)

## Vision and Light

- **Lightly Obscured** (dim light, patchy fog, moderate foliage):
  Disadvantage on sight-based Wisdom (Perception) checks.
- **Heavily Obscured** (darkness, heavy fog, dense foliage): you gain the
  Blinded condition for anything you try to see there.
- **Bright Light:** normal vision. **Dim Light:** creates a Lightly
  Obscured area (twilight, dawn, boundary of a light source). **Darkness:**
  creates a Heavily Obscured area.
- **Special senses** (Blindsight, Darkvision, Tremorsense, Truesight) let
  specific creatures perceive despite these conditions, within their
  stated range.

## Hiding

The GM decides when hiding is possible; you take the Hide action to try
(a Dexterity (Stealth) check).

## Interacting with Objects

An object is a discrete, inanimate item (not a building or vehicle, which
are made of many objects). In combat/time-pressured situations, you get
**one free object interaction per turn** during your move or action;
further interactions require the Utilize action.

- **Finding hidden objects:** searching near a hidden object typically
  calls for a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Carrying objects:** you generally don't need to track weight unless
  hauling something unusually heavy/bulky (carrying-capacity rules apply
  then).
- **Breaking objects:** a fragile nonmagical object can be destroyed
  automatically as an action; tougher objects use the object-breaking
  rules (AC/HP set by the GM).

## Hazards

The general rules reference (but leave fully detailed in the Rules
Glossary, not included in this skill's dataset): Burning, Dehydration,
Falling, Malnutrition, Suffocation.

## Travel

For long trips, the GM can just narrate travel time, or use the pace
rules below when exact timing matters. For movement in the middle of a
tense scene (every second counts), use the combat movement rules instead.

### Travel Pace

| Pace   | Per Minute  | Per Hour | Per Day  | Effect                                                       |
|--------|--------------|----------|----------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fast   | 120 meters   | 4 miles  | 30 miles | Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception/Survival) and Dex (Stealth)    |
| Normal | 90 meters    | 3 miles  | 24 miles | Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks                        |
| Slow   | 60 meters    | 2 miles  | 18 miles | Advantage on Wisdom (Perception/Survival) checks                  |

Mounted travelers can cover double the distance for 1 hour, then the
mounts need a Short or Long Rest before repeating that pace. Vehicle
travelers on land choose a pace normally; waterborne travelers are capped
at the vessel's speed (ships might run up to 24 hours/day depending on
crew).

## Marching Order

Establish a marching order while traveling (indoors or outdoors) — it
determines who's affected by traps, who spots hidden enemies first, and
who's closest if a fight breaks out. Can be changed outside combat.

## Resting

Any creature can take an hour-long **Short Rest** during the day and an
8-hour **Long Rest** to end the day; regaining Hit Points is a main
benefit of resting (full mechanical detail — like Hit Dice spending on a
Short Rest, or what a Long Rest resets — lives in the Rules Glossary,
which isn't part of this skill's dataset; treat a Long Rest as fully
restoring HP and expended resources by default unless a class feature
says otherwise).
