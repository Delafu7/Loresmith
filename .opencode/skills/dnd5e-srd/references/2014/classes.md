# Classes — Character Creation Overview (SRD 5.1)

Quick reference for building a character: hit die, saving throws, 
proficiencies, and available subclasses per class. For the full text 
of a specific class feature, use `scripts/query.py features <name>`. 
For level-by-level progression (spell slots, when features are gained), 
use `scripts/query.py levels <class>-<level>` (e.g. `wizard-5`).

## Barbarian

- **Hit Die:** d12
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** STR, CON
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields, Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons, Saving Throw: STR, Saving Throw: CON
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, and Survival
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Berserker

## Bard

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** DEX, CHA
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Simple Weapons, Longswords, Rapiers, Shortswords, Hand crossbows, Saving Throw: DEX, Saving Throw: CHA
- **Skill choices:** Choose any three
- **Skill choices:** Three musical instruments of your choice
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Lore

## Cleric

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** WIS, CHA
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields, Simple Weapons, Saving Throw: WIS, Saving Throw: CHA
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from History, Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Life

## Druid

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** INT, WIS
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields, Clubs, Daggers, Javelins, Maces, Quarterstaffs, Sickles, Spears, Darts, Slings, Scimitars, Herbalism Kit, Saving Throw: INT, Saving Throw: WIS
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Arcana, Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Religion, and Survival
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Land

## Fighter

- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** STR, CON
- **Proficiencies:** All armor, Shields, Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons, Saving Throw: STR, Saving Throw: CON
- **Skill choices:** Choose two skills from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, and Survival
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Champion

## Monk

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** STR, DEX
- **Proficiencies:** Simple Weapons, Shortswords, Saving Throw: DEX, Saving Throw: STR
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, and Stealth
- **Skill choices:** Choose one type of artisan’s tools or one musical instrument
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Open Hand

## Paladin

- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** WIS, CHA
- **Proficiencies:** All armor, Shields, Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons, Saving Throw: WIS, Saving Throw: CHA
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Devotion

## Ranger

- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** STR, DEX
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields, Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons, Saving Throw: DEX, Saving Throw: STR
- **Skill choices:** Choose three from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, and Survival
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Hunter

## Rogue

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** DEX, INT
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Simple Weapons, Longswords, Rapiers, Shortswords, Hand crossbows, Thieves' Tools, Saving Throw: DEX, Saving Throw: INT
- **Skill choices:** Choose four from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Thief

## Sorcerer

- **Hit Die:** d6
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** CON, CHA
- **Proficiencies:** Daggers, Darts, Slings, Quarterstaffs, Crossbows, light, Saving Throw: CON, Saving Throw: CHA
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Arcana, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion, and Religion
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Draconic

## Warlock

- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** WIS, CHA
- **Proficiencies:** Light Armor, Simple Weapons, Saving Throw: WIS, Saving Throw: CHA
- **Skill choices:** Choose two skills from Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, and Religion
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Fiend

## Wizard

- **Hit Die:** d6
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** INT, WIS
- **Proficiencies:** Daggers, Darts, Slings, Quarterstaffs, Crossbows, light, Saving Throw: INT, Saving Throw: WIS
- **Skill choices:** Choose two from Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, and Religion
- **Subclasses (SRD):** Evocation
